The one thing you want them to do right now.

Front end just means anything a customer, guest, or visitor taps themselves. It is the single most useful action for that exact spot, right when they are standing in front of it.

Back end — what your team uses

The side your customers never see.

Back end means the same tap, pointed at something private — a link only your staff knows about. Stick one in the stockroom, break room, or on a piece of equipment, and it becomes an internal tool instead of a customer touchpoint.

Staff SOPs & training

Tap it on a piece of equipment or station to open the how-to guide, safety steps, or a quick training video for new hires.

Onboarding

Shift handoff & notes

Point it at a shared doc or form so the closing shift can log what the opening shift needs to know.

Communication

Inventory & reorder

Stick one on a shelf or supply bin that opens a reorder form or a running low-stock checklist.

Operations

Maintenance logs

Attach one to equipment to log service dates, report issues, or pull up the manual on the spot.

Upkeep

Same card either way — the only difference is what you point it at, and only you can change it.

Simple ownership

Change the link yourself.

Every Mini Tap includes a how-to card. A staff member can update its destination from a compatible smartphone without installing an app, and the printed QR code keeps a fallback available.
