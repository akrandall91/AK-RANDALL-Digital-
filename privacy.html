Privacy

Privacy in plain language.

What this site collects, why, and how to have it removed.

What you send

The contact form collects what you type: your name, email, optional phone and business name, and your message. It also notes the page you sent it from and how you found the site, so your request can be understood.

Where it’s kept

Messages are stored in a private Google Sheet that only AK Randall Digital can open. Your information is never sold or added to unrelated marketing lists.

Booking a call

The booking calendar is provided by Google. What you enter there is handled under Google’s terms and is used to create and manage your appointment.

Site measurement

The site records general activity such as page views, traffic source, device type and button clicks. This never includes your name, email, phone number or message.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you’ve sent, email Andrew@AKRandall.com.
