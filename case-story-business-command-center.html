Plain-language guide

What the business terms mean.

The demonstration keeps the full operating detail, while these definitions make the numbers and screens easier to evaluate without sales or operations experience.

Command center
One owner workspace that connects sales, project delivery, invoices, and reporting.
Handoff
The moment a customer, job, or piece of information moves from one step, person, or tool to the next.
Booked value
The total price of accepted work. It is not the same as cash already collected.
Monthly recurring revenue
The monthly value of ongoing work expected to repeat while the agreement remains active.
Win rate
Accepted opportunities divided by all opportunities that received a final yes-or-no decision.
Website session
One visit to the website. The same person can create more than one session.

The real problem

Useful tools can still create a broken operating path.

A form can capture a potential customer. A sheet can store the contact. A pricing tool can calculate a quote. A calendar can hold the next meeting. None of those pieces guarantees that the full customer story survives the handoff—the moment information moves from one step or tool to the next.

The cost of a disconnected system is rarely one dramatic failure. It is the accumulation of small delays, repeated entry, missing context, and decisions made from partial information.

The connected operating path

Build around the decisions the business has to make next.

The system does not make decisions for the owner. It puts the current status, history, financial context, and next action in one place so the owner can decide with less searching and guesswork.

  1. 01 / CAPTURE

    Bring the signal in

    Website leads and assessment context can enter the owner workspace without becoming another isolated inbox.

  2. 02 / QUALIFY

    Decide what deserves pursuit

    The source, notes, next follow-up, and current stage—where the opportunity sits in the sales process—create one visible record of the next move.

  3. 03 / PRICE

    Protect scope and expected profit

    Service levels, optional add-ons, available time, delivery cost, and quote totals connect the price to the work required to deliver it.

  4. 04 / DELIVER

    Carry the promise forward

    An accepted quote can become a project, tasks, invoice, and renewal path without rebuilding the story from scratch.

  5. 05 / LEARN

    See what the system is producing

    Reports and website analytics create a foundation for better questions about demand, conversion, workload, and follow-through.
